Ruined House Hunter

📖 Story
Four days ago the street stopped answering. Gas station, corner store, a church that burned from the inside, a warehouse missing everything but one crate — all of it leads to a house at the end nobody's come back from. You're the last one dumb enough to go looking. Whoever came before you left notes behind; find all six and you'll understand what this street actually is before it decides what you are. Seven waves hold the house between you and the truth, each nastier and smarter than the last — some hold a line, some dig in and wait, some hunt the moment you're in range, and in the worst ones, one spotting you alerts them all. From level 2 on, one climbs to the roof and shoots down at you. Three types swing axes and claws inside the house, the rest shoot at you from the street. Take cover behind cars and broken walls. You start with a pistol, shotgun, sniper rifle, and automatic rifle already in hand — all with unlimited ammo.
