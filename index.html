Ruined House Hunter

Past a burned-out gas station, a gutted corner store, and a collapsed church, a street full of ruined houses leads to a derelict house at the end. From level 2 on, one bot climbs to the roof and shoots down at you. 5 waves hold this ground, each nastier than the last — three of them swing axes inside the house, the rest shoot at you from the street. Take cover behind cars and broken walls. You start with a pistol, shotgun, and sniper rifle already in hand — all with unlimited ammo.
  • 🕹 Move: left joystick or W A S D
  • 👁 Look: drag anywhere on the right half of the screen (or mouse on desktop)
  • 🔦 Flashlight: round button or key F
  • 🔫 Fire: big red button bottom-right or Space
  • 🔃 Reload: 🔃 button or key R
  • 1️⃣2️⃣ Switch weapon: bottom icons or key 1 / 2
  • 💨 Quick dash: amber button or Q — a fast step back, with brief invulnerability
  • 🛷 Slide: blue button or X — a fast forward slide
  • 🧎 Crouch: square button or C — you’re shorter behind cover
  • 💣 Grenade: red button or G — only 3, save them for grouped bots
  • ⬆️ Jump: button next to crouch or J
  • 🎯 Sniper: zoom with 🔭, Z, or right-click — fires either way
  • 🎯 Goal: clear all 5 levels — hunt every bot, then return to the yard gate to advance
