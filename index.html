Ruined House Hunter

Past a burned-out gas station, a gutted corner store, a collapsed church, and an industrial warehouse, a street full of ruined houses leads to a derelict house at the end. From level 2 on, one bot climbs to the roof and shoots down at you. 7 waves hold this ground, each nastier and smarter than the last — some hold a line, some dig in and wait, some hunt you the moment you're in range, and in the worst ones, one spotting you alerts them all. Three types swing axes and claws inside the house, the rest shoot at you from the street. Take cover behind cars and broken walls. You start with a pistol, shotgun, sniper rifle, and automatic rifle already in hand — all with unlimited ammo.
  • 🕹 Move: left joystick, W A S D, or a controller's left stick
  • 👁 Look: drag anywhere on the right half of the screen, mouse, or a controller's right stick
  • 🔦 Flashlight: round button, key F, or controller Back
  • 🔫 Fire: big red button bottom-right, Space, or controller RT — hold for the automatic rifle
  • 🔃 Reload: 🔃 button, key R, or controller X
  • 1️⃣2️⃣3️⃣4️⃣ Switch weapon: bottom icons, keys 1-4, or controller LB/RB
  • 💨 Quick dash: amber button, Q, or controller Y — a fast step back, with brief invulnerability
  • 🛷 Slide: blue button, X, or controller left-stick click — a fast forward slide
  • 🧎 Crouch: square button, C, or controller B — you're shorter behind cover
  • 💣 Grenade: red button, G, or controller D-pad up — only 3, save them for grouped bots
  • ⬆️ Jump: button next to crouch, J, or controller A
  • 🎯 Sniper: zoom with 🔭, Z, right-click, or controller LT — fires either way
  • ⏸ Pause: ⏸️ button, Esc, or controller Start
  • 🎯 Goal: clear all 7 levels — hunt every bot, then return to the yard gate to advance
